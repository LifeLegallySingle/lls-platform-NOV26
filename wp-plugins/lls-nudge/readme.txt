=== LLS Nudge ===
Small, SEO-safe site-wide nudge for Life Legally Single™.
Desktop: slide-in card (bottom-right). Mobile: slim bottom bar. Shown 1.2s after the full page loads (white "Check it for Free" button); once a week max;
hidden on the calculator page, for 60 days after a visit/click, and for logged-in members who already ran the Freedom Tax™.
Settings → LLS Nudge: "Live for all visitors" (off = admins-only preview). Preview any page with ?lls_nudge=preview.
Change copy/link via the `lls_nudge_config` filter.
